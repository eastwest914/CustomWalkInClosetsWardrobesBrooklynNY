Our Story

Bespoke Interiors from Brooklyn

Welcome to Anna Gratia Studia, a closet design studio and custom furniture manufacturer at 185 Van Dyke St, Brooklyn. A well-planned closet changes how you start every day, and we design ours to make that easy.

Whether you want a full walk-in closet, a fitted wardrobe or a set of built-in cabinets, we begin with a consultation and walk you through each option before production starts.

Because we manufacture our own pieces, we control quality, fit and finish from the first sketch to installation. Our team answers your questions about materials, layouts and timelines in plain language.

We proudly serve Brooklyn and nearby New York communities, and we welcome homeowners, designers and builders looking for custom work.
